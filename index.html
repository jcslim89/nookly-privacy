Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nookly handles information when you use the Nookly mobile application.

1. Information stored by Nookly

Nookly is designed primarily as a local-first application. Information you enter about your groceries, inventory, quantities, amounts, expiry dates, shopping lists, and storage locations is currently stored locally on your device.

Nookly does not currently require you to create a Nookly account, and Nookly does not currently provide cloud synchronization or household sharing.

2. Notifications

Nookly may request permission to send notifications. These can be used for features such as expiry reminders and low-stock alerts.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

3. Advertising

Nookly may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device information, advertising identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and related technical information in accordance with their own policies and applicable law.

Where required, Nookly uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

Depending on your location and choices, advertisements may be personalized or non-personalized.

You can review available advertising privacy choices from Nookly's Settings screen where applicable.

4. App Tracking Transparency

On Apple devices, Nookly may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework where applicable.

If this permission is requested, you can allow or deny it. You can subsequently manage tracking permission through your device's privacy settings.

5. In-app purchases

Nookly may offer optional in-app purchases, including an option to remove advertisements and optional tips to support development.

Purchases on iOS are processed by Apple through the App Store. Nookly does not directly receive or store your full payment-card information.

A qualifying Remove Ads purchase can be restored using the Restore Purchase feature.

6. Feedback and support

Nookly includes options that can open a supported email application so that you can contact us with feedback, bug reports, or other enquiries.

If you choose to contact us by email, the information contained in your message and your email address will be processed as necessary to respond to your enquiry.

7. Third-party services

Nookly may use third-party services that process information according to their respective privacy policies. These services may include:

  • Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform
  • Apple App Store and StoreKit
  • Expo services used to build and operate the application

8. Data retention

Grocery and inventory information stored locally by Nookly generally remains on your device until you modify or delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application, subject to device backup and operating-system behaviour.

Information processed by third-party services is subject to the retention practices of those services.

9. Your privacy choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Advertising consent choices can be managed through Nookly where applicable. Device-level permissions, including notifications and tracking permissions, can be managed through your device settings.

10. Children's privacy

Nookly is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children through Nookly.

11. International processing

Third-party service providers used by Nookly may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where applicable, those providers are responsible for using appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to design Nookly with privacy and security in mind. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nookly changes or as legal and regulatory requirements change. The date at the top of this page will be updated when the policy is revised.
