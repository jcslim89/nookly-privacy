About Nookly

Nookly helps you keep track of groceries, stock levels, expiry dates, storage locations, and your shopping list.

Purchases

Nookly offers an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase and optional tips to support the developer.

If you've previously purchased Remove Ads, open Nookly and go to Settings → Support Nookly → Restore Purchase.

Notifications

Nookly can send notifications about groceries that are expiring or running low. Notification permissions can be managed in your device settings.
